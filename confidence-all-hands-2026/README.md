# Confidence 2026: Our Impact, Our Future (Year-End All Hands planning proposal)

Planning proposal deck for Confidence leadership, group leads and pod leads. It sets up the year-end All Hands (target Nov 23 or 24, 2026) and the decisions needed to start planning. Travis Kubicek is proposed as planning lead.

- `Confidence-2026-All-Hands-Planning-Proposal.pptx`: the deck. 17 main slides plus a 4-slide appendix, with speaker notes on every slide and PowerPoint sections (Proposal, The meeting, Planning, Decisions, Appendix).
- `build/build_deck.py`: generates the deck on the VML 2026 template (`python3 build/build_deck.py`).
- `template/VML-Template-Feb-2026.pptx`: the VML presentation template (light and dark layouts, Snowflake Sans theme fonts).
- `template/VML2026.thmx`: the VML 2026 theme. It matches the template's own theme.

Deck flow: the proposal → today's three decisions → why this format → run of show → opening, leadership updates and close → Our Impact (featured stories, themes and breadth) → Our People → the Confidence Future 3 → popcorn conversation → roles → timeline → what we need from leads → risks → decisions → closing statement.

Appendix: objectives, content targets and measures of success · open questions (all 18) · source material.

Before presenting:
- Install Snowflake Sans (my.vml.com). Without it, PowerPoint substitutes another font and spacing will shift.
- When exporting to PDF, choose "Best for printing" so the brand fonts are kept.
- Before any figures move into the All Hands deck itself: reconcile the Sherwin-Williams scope (pods vs. teams vs. workstreams) and confirm the Mazda Oct 20 and 27 milestones.
