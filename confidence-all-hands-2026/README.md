# Confidence 2026: Our Impact, Our Future (Year-End All Hands overview)

A four-slide overview of the format and content of the Confidence year-end All Hands. It gives leadership, group leads and pod leads enough context to react and give feedback. Planning detail lives elsewhere.

- `Confidence-2026-All-Hands-Overview.pptx`: the deck, with short speaker notes on every slide.
- `build/build_deck.py`: generates the deck on the VML 2026 template (`python3 build/build_deck.py`).
- `template/VML-Template-Feb-2026.pptx`: the VML presentation template (light and dark layouts, Snowflake Sans theme fonts).
- `template/VML2026.thmx`: the VML 2026 theme. It matches the template's own theme.

Slides:
1. Overview: the idea and its three parts (Our Impact, Our People, Our Future)
2. Format: a proposed 60-minute flow
3. Content: what each part could include, with examples
4. We'd like your reactions: four feedback prompts

Install Snowflake Sans (my.vml.com) before presenting. When exporting to PDF, choose "Best for printing."
