# Confidence 2026: Our Impact, Our Future (Year-End All Hands content outline)

A five-slide outline of what Kylie has in mind for the content of the Confidence year-end All Hands: the intent, a proposed flow, and examples and thought starters for each part. It isn't a full plan; planning detail (roles, timeline, logistics) lives elsewhere.

- `Confidence-2026-All-Hands-Content-Outline.pptx`: the deck, with speaker notes on every slide.
- `build/build_deck.py`: generates the deck on the VML 2026 template (`python3 build/build_deck.py`).
- `template/VML-Template-Feb-2026.pptx`: the VML presentation template (light and dark layouts, Snowflake Sans theme fonts).
- `template/VML2026.thmx`: the VML 2026 theme. It matches the template's own theme.

Slides:
1. What Kylie has in mind: the idea, three intentions, format
2. The outline: a 60-minute flow with the intention behind each segment
3. Our Impact: intention, organizing by theme, example stories and breadth
4. Our People: intention, thought starters, example spotlights
5. Our Future: the Confidence Future 3 provocations, the popcorn conversation and prompts

Notes:
- Install Snowflake Sans (my.vml.com) before presenting. When exporting to PDF, choose "Best for printing."
- Example figures come from the 2026 Technology Reports and still need validating.
